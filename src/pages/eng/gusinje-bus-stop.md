Hi. You are probably looking for the same thing I was: where the bus actually stops in Gusinje.

I walked down from Vusanje to Gusinje and then spent a while trying to find the bus. There is no station, no shelter, no sign, no timetable on a pole. Every guide online says "take the bus from Gusinje" and stops there. So here is the exact spot where I found it.

## The bus stop

No shelter, no sign, just a wide bit of the main road through Gusinje. The route starts here, so there is nothing to flag down.

**42.56583, 19.83322** — [open the pin in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=42.56583,19.83322)

The bus that pulls in here is Elan Company, heading to Plav and on to Podgorica.

## Gusinje to Podgorica bus

The bus that pulls in at this stop runs to Podgorica four times a day, calling at Plav first.

**Departure times**

- **05:46** — Elan Company
- **12:21** — Elan Company
- **15:45** — Elan Company
- **16:45** — Arena Tours

Four a day, all of them through Plav first.

## Why a 47 km trip takes four hours

Because the bus does not go that way. There is no bus route straight over the mountains to Podgorica. It loops north through Plav, Berane, Mojkovac and Kolasin, about 195 km of road. Driving the direct way takes under two hours.

## Tickets

Two options. Buy online at [busticket4.me](https://busticket4.me), which cost me 15.80 euros, and print the ticket before you travel. Or pay the driver in cash when you board. Luggage is 2 euros extra, also to the driver.

## Worth saying out loud at the stop

**Da li cekate autobus za Podgoricu?** — Are you waiting for the bus to Podgorica?

**Odakle polazi autobus za Podgoricu?** — Where does the bus to Podgorica leave from?

## Quick answers

**Is there a bus station in Gusinje?**
No. Map apps show a point called "Autobuska stanica Gusinje" but there is no building, no ticket office and no staff. It is a piece of road.

**What time does the bus leave Gusinje?**
Four a day. 05:46, 12:21 and 15:45 with Elan Company, then 16:45 with Arena Tours. All of them call at Plav first.

**How long does the bus from Gusinje to Podgorica take?**
About four hours. The direct distance is only 47 km, but there is no bus route over the mountains, so it loops north through Plav, Berane, Mojkovac and Kolasin, roughly 195 km of road.

**How much is the ticket from Gusinje to Podgorica?**
Mine cost 15.80 euros online at busticket4.me. You can also pay the driver in cash when you board. Luggage is 2 euros extra, also to the driver.

**Is there a bus from Gusinje to Plav?**
Yes. All four services call at Plav first, so any of them will get you there. It is the first stop out of Gusinje.

**Which company runs the bus?**
Elan Company, to Plav and Podgorica.

**Can I buy the ticket online?**
Yes, at busticket4.me. It costs about 15.80 euros and has to be printed before you travel. Otherwise pay the driver in cash on board.
